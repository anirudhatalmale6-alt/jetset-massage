About Jetset Massage

We Elevate Relaxation With A Seamless Blend Of Comfort Convenience And Care

Experience the ultimate in relaxation and well-being with Jetset Massage, where professional mobile massage services bring personalized treatments directly to you. Enjoy the comfort of your own space while skilled therapists deliver tailored massage experiences at your home, hotel, office, fitness studio, wellness space, or event, creating a calm and restorative environment wherever you are. Our approach is simple, combining expert care, convenience, and attention to your individual needs. Whether you are looking to ease tension, recover with targeted treatment, support post-workout recovery, or take time to unwind, each session is delivered with professionalism and care to support your overall well-being.

Our Services

Massages Crafted To Relieve Tension
Restore Balance And Rejuvenate Your Body

Deep Tissue Massage

Target chronic muscle tension with firm, focused pressure. Ideal for athletes, travelers, or those dealing with stiffness.

Swedish Massage

Gentle and rhythmic strokes to promote circulation, reduce stress, and encourage full-body relaxation.

Sports Massage

Tailored for active lifestyles. Combines stretching, compression, and trigger point work to support performance and recovery.

Couples / Duo Massage

Experience relaxation side by side — perfect for couples, friends, or shared pampering sessions.

Chair Massage

An express neck & shoulder focused treatment designed to relieve tension in the neck, shoulders, and upper back, ideal for professionals, athletes, performance & recovery, and busy schedules.

Reflexology Massage

Treats soft tissue aches, pain, and injuries that are associated with recreational activities. It reduces muscle stiffness and improves relaxation.
