Deep Tissue Massage

What Is Deep Tissue Massage?

Deep tissue massage uses slow, deliberate strokes and focused finger pressure to target the innermost layers of your muscles and connective tissues. Unlike Swedish massage, deep tissue work concentrates on specific problem areas, making it an excellent choice for chronic pain relief and injury recovery.

Benefits of Deep Tissue Massage

  • Relieves chronic back pain and muscle tension
  • Breaks up scar tissue and adhesions
  • Improves range of motion and flexibility
  • Reduces inflammation and promotes healing
  • Lowers blood pressure and heart rate

Ideal For

  • Athletes and active individuals
  • Those with chronic pain or stiffness
  • Travelers recovering from long flights
  • Desk workers with postural issues
  • Residents of luxury buildings with on-site fitness centers and wellness amenities
